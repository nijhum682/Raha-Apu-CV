About Me

Biography

I am a Computer Science and Engineering graduate from Khulna University of Engineering & Technology (KUET), passionate about bridging academic research with practical AI applications. My core expertise lies at the intersection of Deep Learning, Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning, and TinyML.

Currently, I serve as a Part-time Lecturer in the CSE Department at Southeast University, where I enjoy mentoring undergraduates, alongside working as a Research Assistant for the PPFL Project at United International University. Through these roles, I aim to advance privacy protection in machine learning systems, enabling decentralized devices to train models without compromising user confidentiality.
